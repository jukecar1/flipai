What if the dinosaur-killing asteroid hit today?

You're walking down a Manhattan avenue, about 1,600 miles from where a 10-kilometer asteroid hits Mexico, and you see nothing. Five minutes later the ground starts to move. Scientists estimate the shaking at magnitude 9 to 11, glass and brick rain onto the street, and then the sky begins to glow as rock thrown into space falls back through the atmosphere. Models show that heat can ignite fires worldwide. The scenes of damage here are illustrative, but the numbers come from published studies. Would you know what was happening?
