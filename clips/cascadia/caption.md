What if the Cascadia Subduction Zone ruptured?

You'd have roughly 15 to 20 minutes before the first waves arrive. It last happened in 1700, and scientists estimate roughly a 10 to 15 percent chance of another full rupture in the next 50 years. Would you make it to high ground?
