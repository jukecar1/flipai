**Title:** What happens in the first hours after a president is assassinated? 🇺🇸

**Caption:**
There's no gap in power. The moment a president dies, the Vice President becomes President, and the oath that follows is just the formal step. Here's the order of events, based on the law and on what happened in 1963. 🕯️

This is a hypothetical explainer about the office, not a prediction. Nothing in the video shows an attack or any real person.

What the video covers:
• Minute 0: under the 25th Amendment (Section 1), the Vice President becomes President when a president dies. The oath is customary and usually comes within hours.
• Precedent: Lyndon Johnson was sworn in at 2:38 pm on Nov 22, 1963, 1 hour 38 minutes after Kennedy was pronounced dead at 1:00 pm.
• Markets: the NYSE closed early (2:07 pm) on Nov 22, 1963 and stayed shut for the funeral. When it reopened on Nov 26 the Dow rose about 4.5%.
• Mourning: flags fly at half-staff for 30 days after a president's death (4 U.S.C. § 7).
• The Vice President's office: the new president nominates a replacement and both houses of Congress confirm by majority (25th Amendment, Section 2, used for Ford in 1973 and Rockefeller in 1974).
• If more than one office were empty, the Presidential Succession Act orders the line: Speaker of the House, then the Senate's President pro tempore, then the Cabinet.
• History: eight presidents have died in office, and four were assassinated: Lincoln, Garfield, McKinley and Kennedy.
