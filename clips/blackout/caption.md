What if all internet and data went down forever?

You're looking out over a city at 6 PM when every network goes dark at once. Cards and ATMs stop working first, because most money only exists as data. Flights are grounded, stores run low (most hold about three days of food), fuel stops flowing, and power and water systems that run over networks start to fail. Hospital generators typically hold about four days of fuel. With emergency dispatch down, fires burn unchecked. We've never lost the internet everywhere at once, but a single bad software update grounded thousands of flights in 2024. Would your city last a month?
