**Title:** What if you drove to the Moon? 🚗🌕

**Caption:**
Cruise control on, 65 mph, no stops. The Moon is 238,855 miles away, so that's about 153 days of nonstop driving. You'd pass the space station on day 1 and the GPS satellites on day 8. Nobody stops for gas.

Then you get home, park by the beach… and the ground starts shaking. 😳 (The video is time-compressed and illustrative.)

The numbers:
• Earth to Moon: 238,855 miles on average, center to center (it ranges from about 225,600 to 252,000 miles)
• At 65 mph: 238,855 ÷ 65 ÷ 24 = about 153 days. The round trip is about 306 days. Even at 120 mph it's about 83 days (my arithmetic)
• Gas: roughly 9,550 gallons at 25 mpg, about $30,000 at $3.10 a gallon (my estimate)
• The road passes the ISS at about 250 miles up, the GPS satellites at about 12,550 miles and geostationary orbit at about 22,236 miles
• A tsunami moves 500+ mph in deep water, like a jet, and slows to about 20–30 mph near shore, but the water keeps piling up (NOAA)
• If the ground shakes for a long time near the coast, or the sea suddenly pulls back, go uphill or inland right away. Don't wait for a siren.
