**Title:** Evolution of Los Angeles: 3D city timelapse (1781–2026) 🌴

**Caption:**
From a village of 44 settlers to a city of almost 4 million. Watch Los Angeles grow across 245 years on a real map: the river, the railroads, the oil boom, Hollywood, the freeways and the skyline. 🗺️

This is a modeled timelapse. The terrain, streets, buildings and parks are real (OpenStreetMap), but the year each one appears is an estimate, not an actual construction date.

What's in it:
• 1781: El Pueblo de Los Ángeles is founded on Sept 4 by 44 settlers
• 1850: Los Angeles is incorporated (April 4) and California joins the Union (Sept 9)
• 1892: Edward Doheny strikes oil near Echo Park
• 1910: Hollywood is annexed. 1913: the Los Angeles Aqueduct opens. 1915: the San Fernando Valley joins the city
• 1923: the Hollywood sign goes up. 1928: Mines Field opens (renamed LAX in 1949)
• 1932: the Olympics come to the Coliseum. 1940: the Arroyo Seco Parkway opens, the first freeway in the West (Dec 30)
• 1955: Disneyland opens in Anaheim (July 17). 1962: Dodger Stadium opens
• 1984: the Olympics return. 1990: Metro Rail's Blue Line opens
• 2017: the Wilshire Grand Center opens at 1,100 ft, the tallest building west of the Mississippi. 2020: SoFi Stadium opens
• Population (city of Los Angeles, Census): 1,610 in 1850 → 102,479 in 1900 → 1,238,048 in 1930 → about 3.9 million in 2020
• Next: the 2028 Olympics
