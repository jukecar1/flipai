What if the dinosaur-killing asteroid hit New York?

You're walking down a Manhattan avenue with a coffee when the sky starts to glow. A 10-kilometer asteroid moving about 45,000 mph gives you roughly seven seconds of warning. Manhattan would sit inside the fireball (about 136 km across) and the crater would be around 120 km wide, so no one nearby would survive. The slowed scene in the middle is an illustration. Worldwide: a heat pulse like an oven on broil for about an hour, years of darkness, and about 75% of species lost, as 66 million years ago. NASA knows of no asteroid this size that threatens Earth for 100+ years. Would you look up?
