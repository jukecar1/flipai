# Pitch scripts: "I already built it, look at it for free"

**Why this works:** a service has to be bought, but a finished thing only has to be looked at.
Keep three honesty rules and everything below stays trustworthy:
1. Say it was built from their **public** Google info and photos.
2. The link is unlisted, and you take it down the moment they say no.
3. No fake urgency. Leave it up a week, not "a day".

## 1. Cold call (about 30 seconds)
> "Hi, is this [Name]? I'm [Your name], a local web designer here in [town]. I came across [Business] on Google, and you have great reviews but no website. So I built you one using your public Google info. You can look at it for free. Can I text you the link?"

If yes: "Perfect. I'll leave it up for a week. I'll call tomorrow to hear what you think, and if it's not for you I'll take it down. No hard feelings."

## 2. Voicemail
> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name], a local web designer. I built a free sample website for [Business] from your Google reviews and photos. I'll text you the link. Take a look when you have a minute, and call me back at [number] if you want to talk. Thanks."

## 3. Text with the link
> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from the call. Here's the sample site I built for [Business]: [link]. It stays up for a week, and I'll delete it any time if you're not interested. Let me know what you think."

## 4. Walk-in (best for local shops)
Show it on your phone before you say anything about price.
> "I'm a local web designer. I made a sample site for [Business] from your Google profile. Can I show you on my phone? It takes a minute."

## 5. Follow-up call, next day
> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name]. Did you get a chance to look at the site? ... What did you like or want changed?"

Listen first. Change one thing on the spot if they ask. Do not mention price until they say they like it.

## 6. When they like it: the price
> "I make one simple package. It's a [$400] build, and since you'd be one of my first clients I can do [$200] if you let me show your site as an example and give me a short testimonial. Then it's $50 a month, which covers hosting, up to two edits a month, keeping the contact form working, and a quarterly Google profile check. Month-to-month, and you keep your domain. Want me to put it live this week?"

Send the one-page offer PDF right after. Mention add-ons last, in one sentence.

## 7. Objections
- **"I already have Facebook/Google."** "Those are great, and a site you own adds more. It shows up for searches like [service] in [town], it's the first place customers check, and it doesn't depend on Google keeping your listing."
- **"How much?"** Give the price from step 6. Don't discount first. Ask what part isn't clear.
- **"Let me think about it."** "Of course. I'll leave the site up through [date]. Would it help if I sent the details in writing?"
- **"Who are you? How do I know this is legit?"** "I'm local, here's my number, and I can come by. You owe nothing for looking, and you keep your domain."
- **"Not interested."** "No problem, I'll delete the site today. Thanks for looking." Then actually delete it and mark it in the tracker.
- **"Can you change X?"** "Yes." Offer one revision before launch.

## 8. After the call
Log it in the tracker the same day: Channel, Date contacted, Replied, Draft shown, Status.
